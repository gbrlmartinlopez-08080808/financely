Gamify reading, one jump at a time.

Frogity is a free reader for the files you already have: PDFs, books, forms and scans. Read a little every day and a frog with a jetpack lights up your map, one yellow @ per day you reach your goal.

A calm reader that also keeps you coming back

Everything runs on your phone. Open anything, read it your way, and let the streak do the nagging.

read

Liquid mode for PDFs

Fixed pages reflow into one column that fits your phone. Equations, tables and figures stay intact.

listen

Read-aloud that keeps playing

Natural voices with a highlight that follows along, a media notification and lock-screen controls. It keeps going with the screen off.

forms

Fill and sign forms

Frogity spots forms, lists every field in order, fills what it knows from your saved details, and lets you sign with a saved signature. Export a flattened PDF.

scan

Scans and photos, read in the background

On-device text recognition makes scans searchable and listenable, with a small progress badge instead of a blocking screen.

look up

Words, origins, pictures

Press and hold a word for its definition, roots and a Wikipedia card.

free books

Find free reading

Browse public-domain and open-access books and papers, from classics to more recent work, and add them to your shelf in a tap.

share

Share a book as a card

Turn a cover into a picture: the book in a frosted acrylic holder, with your streak.

widget

Home-screen widget

Your streak, the last seven days and today’s progress, in ASCII.

ask

Ask your document (optional)

Summaries and explanations from a free AI service. Off until you turn it on, and it says exactly what is sent.

Streaks that scale to you

Set your own daily goal. Every mark in the calendar is measured against it, one freeze day a week saves a missed day, and you can earn more.

What the marks mean

For a 15-minute goal:

· 0: 1+ 4* 8% 12@ 15~ freeze

Change the goal and the minutes behind each mark change with it.

The reward

Reach your goal and the next morning a frog with a jetpack flies across your night sky. The more days you’ve kept your word, the further it carries them, then it lands and your map lights up.

  • A day counts after one minute of reading or listening
  • One free freeze day each week, more can be earned
  • Optional evening reminder, made on your phone

Privacy

Frogity has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your library, reading history, streaks, form details and signature stay on your phone and are never sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
