Forms, filled in seconds
Frogity spots a form, lists every field in order, fills what it knows from your saved details and lets you sign with a signature you saved once. Export a flattened PDF — nothing is uploaded.
  • Works on fillable and flat (printed-blank) forms
  • Signature field expands into a signing pad
  • Save, share or add the filled copy to your shelf

Listening that keeps going
Natural read-aloud with a highlight that follows along. Lock the screen or switch apps: it keeps playing, with a media notification and lock-screen controls.
  • Speed ruler, voice picker, sleep timer, focus mode
  • Optional studio voices, downloaded once
  • A foreground media service runs only while you listen

Liquid mode for PDFs
Fixed pages reflow into one column that fits your phone — no pinching, no side-scrolling. Equations, tables and figures stay intact, so maths keeps its structure.
  • Two-column papers, footnotes, hyphenation
  • Pinch-zoom on original pages stays buttery
  • Twelve fonts, four reading themes

Why the app asks for…

    
INTERNET
Word lookups, saving links, Find free books, optional Ask and optional voice downloads. Nothing runs in the background to upload data.

    
POST_NOTIFICATIONS
The optional evening reminder and the listening player’s notification. It is asked only when you switch one of them on. Reminders are scheduled on the phone, not by a server.

    
FOREGROUND_SERVICE · media playback
Keeps read-aloud playing when the screen turns off or you leave the app, with a media notification and lock-screen controls (a standard media session). It starts only when you press play and stops when you pause, finish or dismiss it. It is not used for location, sync or anything else.

    
VIBRATE
Light haptic feedback. Can be switched off in Settings.

    
Files
Documents are opened through Android’s own file picker, so Frogity does not request “all files access”. It has no access to location, contacts, microphone, camera roll or an advertising ID.

    
Is the AI feature safe?
It is optional and off by default. When you turn it on, the app tells you what is sent (your question and short excerpts of the open document). Every answer has a “Report this answer” button that opens an email to support.
