**Title (≤30):** Frogity – Reader & PDF

**Short description (≤80):**

**Full description (≤4000):**
Gamify reading, one jump at a time.

Frogity is a quiet reader for the files you already have: PDFs, EPUB books, Word and OpenDocument files, Markdown, web pages, spreadsheets, slides, photos and scans. Read daily and watch a little ASCII world fill with light — one yellow @ for every day you reach your goal.

STREAKS THAT FORGIVE
Set a daily goal in minutes. Every mark in the streak calendar (: + * % @) is scaled to YOUR goal. One freeze day a week quietly saves your streak when life gets in the way, and you can earn more.
A jetpack frog flies across your map each morning, trailing an @ for every light you've earned.
A home-screen widget shows your streak, the last seven days and today's progress.

LIQUID MODE FOR PDFS
Fixed pages reflow into one column that fits your phone — no pinching, no side-scrolling. Equations, tables and figures stay intact.

LISTEN — EVEN WITH THE SCREEN OFF
Natural read-aloud with a highlight that follows along. It plays like a track: notification and lock-screen controls, and it keeps going when the phone is locked or the app is in the background. Speed ruler, voice picker, sleep timer, focus mode, and optional studio voices.

FILL FORMS IN SECONDS
Open a form and Frogity spots it, lists every field in neat order and fills what it knows from your saved details. Tap the signature field to expand a signing pad, save your signature for next time, then export or share the finished PDF. Works on your phone, nothing uploaded.

LOOK THINGS UP
Press and hold any word for its definition, its origin and a Wikipedia card with a picture. Works in PDF pages and in photos you've scanned.

ASK YOUR DOCUMENT (optional, free)
Summarise, explain or quiz yourself, with formulas typeset properly. Off until you turn it on.

SAVE & SHARE ANYTHING
Add files without copying them, link whole folders, save web pages as clean readable articles, paste notes, or scan paper with on-device text recognition (it keeps reading scans in the background). Share any book as a card: its cover in a frosted acrylic holder with your streak.
Find free books in public-domain and open collections, from classics to more recent works.

CONVERT
Turn documents into PDF, text, Markdown, web pages or images.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your library lives on your phone. No account. No ads. No tracking. Twelve fonts, four reading themes, bookmarks, highlights.

Contains an optional AI feature that sends your question and short excerpts to a third-party service only after you opt in. Needs the notification permission only for the optional evening reminder and the listening player.
