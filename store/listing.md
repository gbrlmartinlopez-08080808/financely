**Full description (≤4000):**
Open anything. Read it beautifully. Pay for nothing.

[App name] is a minimal reader for the files you already have: PDFs, EPUB books, Word and OpenDocument files, Markdown, web pages, spreadsheets, slides, photos and scans.

LIQUID MODE FOR PDFS
Fixed pages reflow into one column that fits your phone — no pinching, no side-scrolling. Equations, tables and figures stay intact, so maths keeps its structure.

LISTEN
Natural read-aloud with a highlight that follows along. Speed ruler, voice picker, sleep timer and a focus mode.

LOOK THINGS UP
Press and hold any word for its definition, its origin (Greek and Latin roots included) and a Wikipedia card with a picture. Works in PDF pages and in photos you've scanned.

ASK YOUR DOCUMENT (optional, free)
Summarise, explain or quiz yourself, with formulas typeset properly. Off until you turn it on.

SAVE ANYTHING
Add files without copying them, link whole folders, save web pages as clean readable articles, paste notes, or scan paper with on-device text recognition.

CONVERT
Turn documents into PDF, text, Markdown, web pages or images.

MADE FOR RETURNING
A quiet reading streak, a spinning ASCII globe that lights one place for every day you read, and short messages that notice your week. Twelve fonts, four reading themes, bookmarks and highlights.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your library lives on your phone. No account. No ads. No tracking.

Contains an optional AI feature that sends your question and short excerpts to a third-party service only after you opt in.
